## 1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Rubber duck spawning on level start inside the starting truck and across facility rooms.
- Configurable `SpawnMode` option (`Both`, `TruckOnly`, `LevelOnly`).
- Configurable `TruckDuckCount` (default: 15, range: 1-50) and `LevelDuckCount` (default: 30, range: 1-100).
- Natural room placement on tables, desks, and shelves using `ValuableVolume` anchors.
- Host-authoritative spawning with Photon synchronization across connected players.
- Custom Thunderstore mod icon fitting R.E.P.O. aesthetic.
